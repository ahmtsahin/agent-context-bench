# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- demonstrating empathy and respect;
- giving and accepting constructive, specific feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the project and community;
- disagreeing with evidence without attacking people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized language or
attention, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, deliberate intimidation,
publishing private information without permission, and conduct that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual publicly represents
the project. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing it. They
may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that violate it, and will explain moderation decisions when
appropriate.

Report abusive or unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer through
GitHub's private reporting mechanisms. If no private channel is available, open
a minimal issue requesting private contact without including sensitive details.
Reports will be reviewed as privately as practical. Retaliation against a
reporter or participant in an investigation is itself a violation.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider context, impact, and pattern of behavior when choosing
a response:

1. **Correction:** a private or public explanation and request to stop.
2. **Warning:** a formal warning with consequences for repeated behavior.
3. **Temporary restriction:** a time-limited ban from project interaction.
4. **Permanent restriction:** removal for sustained abuse, harassment, or
   repeated violations.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
